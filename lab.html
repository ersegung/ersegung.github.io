FSI–NLD Laboratory

Fluid-Structure Interactions
and Nonlinear Dynamics

We study the motion and loading of structures in waves, currents, and ice. Our work brings together laboratory experiments, computational models, and the analysis of complex dynamical systems.

Recirculating water channel

HIG 109 · University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa

The recirculating channel supports experiments on flexible cylinders and other bodies in steady flow. These tests help us examine vortex-induced vibrations, changes in structural modes, and the effects of surface properties and boundary conditions.

Wave flume

HIG 151 · University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa

Wave-flume studies examine wave energy devices, floating flexible structures, and wave-induced motion. High-speed cameras and motion tracking allow us to measure responses and compare them with analytical and numerical predictions.

Research connections

The laboratory is part of the Department of Ocean and Resources Engineering. We work with the Hawaiʻi Marine Energy Center and collaborators in ocean engineering, structural dynamics, and cold regions research.
