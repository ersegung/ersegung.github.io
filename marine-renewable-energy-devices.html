A closer look

What does a PTO damper do?

Try a single, harmonically forced oscillator. A damper can extract energy from its motion, but changing the damping also changes that motion. This is a mechanics lesson, not a complete wave-energy converter model.

For the incoming resource, return to the wave and current calculators. For the water motion itself, open the Ocean Waves Lab.

Read the readiness evidence carefully

TRL 1–3 covers basic principles and proof of concept. Levels 4–6 move through laboratory and relevant-environment validation. Levels 7–9 move from operational prototypes toward qualified systems and proven operation. The assessment belongs to a particular system and test record. [EMEC]

Reported sector ranges in the European Commission/JRC summary updated May 21, 2026, drawing on JRC 2025. These ranges are not scores for every device shown here.
