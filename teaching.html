Teaching

My teaching connects the mechanics of fluids and structures with laboratory observations, numerical models, and ocean engineering applications.

ORE 609

Hydrodynamics of Fluid-Body Interaction

We look at hydrodynamic forces and how bodies respond in fluid flow. Along the way, we examine the assumptions behind analytical and numerical models.

ORE 612

Dynamics of Ocean Structures

We study how ocean structures move under fluid loading, from vibration and modal response to the analysis of measured and simulated motion.

ORE 677

Marine Renewable Energy

We explore marine energy resources and conversion systems, with a focus on wave energy, device hydrodynamics, and what experiments can tell us about performance.

Explore the Marine Energy lessons →

Learning through experiments

Water-channel and wave-flume demonstrations help students connect theory with physical behavior. We use motion measurements, data analysis, and numerical exercises to examine how ocean structures respond to waves and currents.
