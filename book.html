I am preparing a book on the hydrodynamics of fluid–body interactions for Springer.

About the project

The project brings together my teaching and research in fluid–body interactions, structural dynamics, and ocean engineering. It focuses on the physical relationships between fluid motion, hydrodynamic loading, and the response of structures.

The manuscript is currently in preparation. Publication details and supporting materials will be added here as they become available.

Related teaching

The subject is closely connected to my graduate courses in hydrodynamics and the dynamics of ocean structures.
